Privacy policy

Jammin Password Manager is a personal app for Windows and Android. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no servers, no account of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your vault stays on your own devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive.

Google user data the app uses

  • Google Drive, app folder only (drive.appdata): the app creates, reads and updates files in its own hidden folder in your Google Drive. It keeps your encrypted vault file there so your devices can sync it. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address (openid, email): only to show which Google account is connected.

The vault is encrypted on your device with your master password before it is uploaded. Your master password never leaves your device, and nobody else, including Google, can open the vault without it.

Sharing

No Google user data is sent to the developer or to anyone else, sold, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage on your devices

On Windows, the Google sign-in is stored in one file sealed with Windows data protection for your Windows account. On Android, Google Play services keeps the permission; the app stores no Google token of its own.

Removing access and data

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. To delete the app's hidden data from your Drive, open Google Drive on the web, then Settings > Manage apps > Jammin Password Manager > Options > Delete hidden app data.
